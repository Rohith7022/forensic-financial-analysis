# Forensic Financial Analysis – Bags Medical Devices
## VCU – Financial Analytics (FIRE 540) · Deloitte Case Study

A **forensic data analytics investigation** into sales commission fraud at Bags Medical Devices Company, based on the Deloitte FAS LLP case study series. Applied data-driven pattern detection to uncover irregular commission payments across sales representatives.

---

## Overview

Given a dataset of sales commission records and informational interview notes from company employees (CEO, sales reps, operations), this project uses R to:

- Load and validate commission data from CSV
- Identify statistical anomalies and outlier patterns
- Visualize the fraud signal in three key graphics
- Build a case narrative supported by data evidence

---

## The Investigation

**Company:** Bags Medical Device Company  
**Issue:** Suspected manipulation of sales commission records  
**Data:** Commission transaction records per sales representative  

Informational interviews with Steve Flanders (CEO), Mitchel Bogart, Carly McFinnagan, Amanda Smith, Willie Johnson, and Barry McMillian informed the fraud hypothesis before quantitative analysis.

---

## Deliverables

- Commission fraud pattern visualized across 3 key graphics
- Statistical summary identifying anomalous transactions
- Narrative connecting data findings to interview evidence

---

## Tech Stack

`R` `tidyverse` `ggplot2` `dplyr` `Quarto`
